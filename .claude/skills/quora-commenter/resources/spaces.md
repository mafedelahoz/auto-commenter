# Target Quora Topics/Spaces Guide

> When writing Quora answers, reflect each topic's audience and norms.
> ⚠️ Unlike subreddits.md, these entries are a **starting draft**, not
> verified against live Quora data — Topic activity, related-question
> volume, and any Space-specific guidelines should be confirmed the first
> time you actually log in and browse each one (Quora Topics don't expose
> subscriber counts or written rules the way subreddits do, so "Core
> Community Rules" below is Quora's sitewide BNBR policy applied to context,
> not a per-Topic rule page).

## Topic-Specific Guides

### Travel

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Sitewide norms applied here** | Very broad audience, mixed question quality (lots of "best places to visit" listicle-bait). Skip vague listicle questions with no criteria — see BATCH.md skip conditions. |
| **Audience** | General public, huge range of experience levels |
| **Good Questions to Answer** | Specific trip-planning questions with real constraints (dates, budget, cities); multi-city itinerary feedback; "how do I book X" process questions |

## Cross-Topic Promotion Notes

- Quora explicitly allows naming a product when it's the genuine answer to
  what's asked — unlike most of the target subreddits, no blanket
  self-promo ban to work around. The constraint here is answer *quality*,
  not the mention itself: an answer that reads as an ad gets collapsed by
  Quora's own "Needs Improvement" system regardless of whether the mention
  was "technically against the rules."
- Mention CuddlyNest by name only when the question is genuinely about
  accommodation/booking comparison or multi-city trip planning — same
  discipline as the Reddit skill, see personalization_quora.md "Natural
  Promotion" pattern.
- Most answers should still be genuinely useful with no product mention —
  the same credibility logic as Reddit applies: over-mentioning erodes the
  account's trustworthiness for when a mention actually lands well.
