## Forum-Specific Notes

### Europe Forums / Asia Forums / South America Forums (general/regional)

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Nature** | Broad regional forums that funnel into country- and city-specific sub-forums. Most real activity happens at the city level (e.g. "Rome Forum", "Bangkok Forum") — treat the regional forum as a directory, find the specific city forum matching an actual thread before replying. |
| **Good Threads to Answer** | Multi-city itinerary questions, "where to stay" questions with real constraints |
| **Risk Note** | Highest Destination Expert density of the target forums — expect fast, knowledgeable pushback if an answer is generic or wrong |

---

### Solo Travel Forum

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Nature** | Cross-destination forum specifically for solo travelers, direct equivalent to r/solotravel in audience |
| **Good Threads to Answer** | Safety-conscious accommodation questions, solo-friendly area questions |

---

### Budget Travel Forum

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Nature** | Cross-destination forum for budget-conscious travelers, equivalent to r/Shoestring in audience |
| **Good Threads to Answer** | Price-expectation questions, budget itinerary sanity checks |
| **Risk Note** | This is the closest audience overlap with CuddlyNest's own value prop — also therefore the thread type most likely to read as an ad if a product mention is included. Extra caution here specifically. |

## Cross-Forum Promotion Notes

**Default: no product mentions on this platform at all.** See the warning
at the top of SKILL.md. If the user has explicitly and separately confirmed
they accept the elevated risk for TripAdvisor:
- Mention CuddlyNest by name only, never a link, only when it is the literal
  direct answer to a booking-comparison question
- Never repeat the same mention across more than one thread in a session
- Treat any moderation signal (removal, warning, flag) as a stop-everything
  event per BATCH.md Termination Condition 5
