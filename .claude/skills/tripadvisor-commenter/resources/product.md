## Core Value

**"One platform to plan and book your whole trip"**
- All-in-one travel booking: hotels, homes, flights, and local attractions in a single search — no jumping between five tabs
- 22M+ stays across 200+ countries, combining inventory from the world's largest travel sites (hotels, villas, serviced apartments, chalets, hostels, B&Bs)
- AI-powered itinerary planning that personalizes stays + flights + activities to your budget and preferences
- Price watchlists to track fluctuations on a trip you're eyeing, plus direct chat with property owners

---

## Target Customer Pain Points

1. "I have Airbnb, Booking.com, and a flights tab all open just to plan one trip"
2. "I booked on one site and found the same place cheaper somewhere else the next day"
3. "Planning a multi-stop trip takes hours of manual research across different apps"
4. "Booking fees keep sneaking up at checkout"

---

## Competitor Comparison

| Competitor | Your Differentiation |
|------------|---------------------|
| **TripAdvisor itself** | TripAdvisor is primarily reviews-first with booking bolted on; CuddlyNest is booking-first with unified search across hotels, homes, flights, and activities in one flow |
| **Airbnb** | CuddlyNest isn't limited to home rentals — hotels, hostels, villas, and B&Bs are all searchable in one place, plus flights and activities alongside stays |
| **Booking.com** | Adds AI-personalized itinerary building (not just a hotel search) and price watchlists so you're not manually refreshing for a better rate |
